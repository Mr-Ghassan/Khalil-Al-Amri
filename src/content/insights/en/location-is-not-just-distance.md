“Great location” — great for whom?

Location isn’t only distance. Before you call it great, ask four questions:

1. **Demand** — do the people you’re targeting (as residents or tenants) want to live here?
2. **Services** — what you actually need: how close is it?
3. **Access** — how do you get in and out at peak times?
4. **What’s coming** — new supply nearby can lift an area, or crowd you out.

Ask all four before you say “great.”
