Most questions I receive start with “what should I buy?” The more useful question comes first: **what do you want from property?**

Property is a means, not the goal. Your answer decides the kind of property — not the other way round.

## Four goals, four kinds of property

- **A home for your family** — comfort today and in ten years, with what you need close by.
- **Monthly income** — a unit tenants actually want, and a net figure that survives every cost.
- **Value that grows over time** — an asset you can afford to wait for, with a clear idea of who buys it later.
- **Live in it now, rent it later** — a balance between your comfort today and a tenant’s needs tomorrow.

## Why the order matters

Each goal has a property that fits it — and an excellent property built for someone else’s goal can wear you down.

> An excellent property that doesn’t fit your goal is still the wrong decision.

Before any decision, write your goal in one sentence. If you like, we can start from there together.
