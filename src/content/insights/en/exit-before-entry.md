You bought. Fine — how will you sell?

Most people only think about getting in. The question that protects your decision is how you get out.

## Three questions before you sign

- **Who is the buyer in a few years?** A family? An investor? Does the unit suit them?
- **How many similar units will be on sale alongside yours?** A whole project selling the same thing at the same time changes the picture.
- **Is renting a real alternative?** If you don’t sell, could you let it on terms that cover you?

Knowing how you’ll leave is what lets you enter with confidence.
