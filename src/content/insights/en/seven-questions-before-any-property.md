Ask the same seven questions of every unit, and the comparison becomes fair.

## The seven

1. **Goal** — what do you want this money to do?
2. **Entry price** — price isn’t cost. What is the full cost?
3. **Income or use** — what’s left net, or how well does it actually serve you?
4. **Growth** — what will be built around it over the coming years?
5. **Risk** — what happens if something changes: rates, demand, or your own situation?
6. **Staying power** — is there a safety margin left after the down payment?
7. **Exit** — who buys it from you in a few years, and is renting a real alternative?

## Why no score?

A single number hides the thing that matters to you. This is a way of thinking: each point has a test question, and the answer depends on the person asking.
