“Buy or rent?” comes up constantly. Asked that way, it has no single answer.

## Four things before the comparison

- **Your goal** — a long-term home, or a stage in life?
- **How long you’ll stay** — realistically, how many years in the same place?
- **Your down payment** — what’s ready today, and what’s left after it?
- **Your safety margin** — is there still a reserve after the payment and the instalment?

## Then we compare

We put the full cost of buying next to renting for the same period, with the same assumptions, and see where you’re more comfortable.

And the old line, “rent is money thrown away”? There’s some truth in it — the rest depends on you.
