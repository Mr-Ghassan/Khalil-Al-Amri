Going to see a project? Take this list with you.

## Inside the unit

- **Finishing** — check the details with your own eyes, not in the brochure.
- **Orientation** — where is the sun in the morning and the afternoon?
- **Ventilation** — does air actually move through the home?
- **Noise** — listen at more than one time of day if you can.

## Around the building

- **Parking** — how many spaces per unit, and where?
- **Services** — how far are the things you need every day?

## Questions to ask on site

Write your questions before you go, and ask for the important answers in writing.
