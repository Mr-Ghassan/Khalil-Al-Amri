“I’ll wait for the market to drop.”

Sometimes waiting is an excellent decision — just not because of “the bottom”. Nobody knows when the bottom is.

## Wait if…

- you have no **safety margin** after the down payment;
- your **goal** isn’t clear yet;
- your **down payment** isn’t ready.

## Don’t wait only because…

someone else says prices will fall. Waiting has a cost too, and it deserves the same calculation as any other decision.

Sometimes the best advice I can give is: wait. What’s making you wait?
