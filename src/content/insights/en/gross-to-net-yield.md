The yield quoted in an advert is usually **gross**: annual rent divided by price. What reaches you is the **net**.

## Line by line

Start from the annual rent and subtract, in order:

1. **Service charges** that recur every year.
2. **Maintenance** — even a new unit has running costs.
3. **Vacancy** — the months without a tenant.
4. **Any entry cost** that wasn’t in the advertised price.

Then divide what’s left by the **full cost**, not the price alone.

## The month nobody counts

Your flat sat empty for two months? Each empty month is roughly 8% of a year’s rent — two months, roughly 17%.

Vacancy belongs in the calculation from day one, not after it happens.
