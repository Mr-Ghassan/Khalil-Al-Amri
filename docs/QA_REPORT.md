## Visual review (human-style, from rendered screenshots)

Fixed during the loop:
1. Horizon line sat on the kumma instead of the eyes → eye line measured on each master cut-out (0.097–0.139) and wired into CSS geometry; verified by DOM measurement (line/eye ratio 0.135 on the home hero).
2. Mobile hero pushed the message below the fold → figure box reduced to 46 svh; statement, lead and both CTAs now visible on a 390×844 first screen.
3. Inner-page horizon crossed the breadcrumb and H1 → line now runs from the outer edge, behind Khalil, fading before the text column.
4. English hero statement wrapped to five lines → English-specific scale.
5. Text-only page heroes showed a stray glow spot → glow moved to the top corner.
6. 404: English column inherited the Arabic display face and mirrored arrow → fixed.
7. QA harness: smooth scrolling prevented programmatic scrolling, so reveal-on-scroll content and lazy images were missing from full-page captures → harness uses instant scroll (not a user-facing bug).

Checked and acceptable: Arabic line breaks (no orphans in hero, headings balanced), numbers and phone in LTR isolates inside RTL, kumma never cropped on any page or width, nothing overlaps Khalil, contact row on one baseline (stacks under 540 px), focus rings visible on dark and light tones, intent tabs keyboard-operable (arrows follow reading direction), form errors announced and linked.
